# Authors and Contributors

PlantaCRISPAI is developed and maintained at the **Division of Plant
Physiology, ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute (ICAR-IARI)**,
Pusa, New Delhi, under the PIU-GET Project (Code 12-197).
